Lesson 33 — Bootstrap Cover Example

This lesson demonstrates Bootstrap’s Cover Template — a simple fullscreen layout perfect for landing pages, hero sections, and minimal home screens.

Learn more

Cover template for Bootstrap by @mdo.

Teaching Notes

1. Fullscreen Background Technique:
We use background-size: cover; to ensure the coral reef image fills the entire viewport without distortion. The fixed keyword keeps the background stationary while content scrolls.

2. Dark Overlay for Readability:
The body::before overlay improves contrast without hiding the background.

3. Centering Content with Flexbox:
The hero section is centered using flexbox, but the body is no longer flex-centered.

4. Max-Width for Readability:
The .cover-container is limited to 42em for optimal line length.

5. Transparent “Glass” Notes Block:
Uses rgba(255,255,255,0.25) and backdrop-filter: blur(6px).

6. Why This Layout Works:
Minimal, centered, and visually striking — ideal for landing pages.

7. Real-World Use Cases:
• App landing pages
• Portfolio intros
• Product announcements
• Coming Soon pages

8. Accessibility Considerations:
Maintain contrast, avoid busy backgrounds behind text.

9. Customization Ideas:
Add gradients, animations, or time‑based background swaps.
