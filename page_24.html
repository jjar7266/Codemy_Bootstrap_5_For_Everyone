Lesson 24 - Containers

.container-sm — 100% wide until the small breakpoint (≥576px)
.container-md — 100% wide until the medium breakpoint (≥768px)
.container-lg — 100% wide until the large breakpoint (≥992px)
.container-xl — 100% wide until the extra-large breakpoint (≥1200px)
.container-xxl — 100% wide until the extra-extra-large breakpoint (≥1400px)
.container-fluid — Always 100% wide
